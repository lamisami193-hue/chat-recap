About TG Wrapped

Last updated: October 23, 2026

TG Wrapped turns a Telegram chat export into a fun, shareable recap, in the spirit of a yearly "wrapped" summary. You choose a chat, and the tool shows message counts, who talks the most, who starts conversations, your longest silence, your busiest hour, and your most used words and emojis, as story cards you can download and share.

Private by design. Your export file is read and analyzed inside your own browser. It is not uploaded to our servers, and your chat content is never sent anywhere. Google Analytics automatically measures visits and a few basic interactions. Your Telegram export, chat names and message contents are never sent to analytics.

How it works

  • In Telegram Desktop, export your data (or a single chat) as machine-readable JSON. Leaving media out keeps the file small.
  • Open TG Wrapped and drop in the file.
  • Pick the chats and period you want, and generate your cards.
  • Download the cards and share them if you like. Use the hide-names option if the chat includes other people.

Who makes it

TG Wrapped is an independent project made by a solo developer. It is free to use. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Telegram. "Telegram" is a trademark of its respective owner and is used here only to describe the files this tool can read.

Languages

The tool is available in English, Arabic, Spanish, French and Portuguese.
